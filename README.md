# Coffee – Retail Store Expansion Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Coffee's sales performance and identifying the most suitable Indian cities for expanding its physical coffee shop business.

Using PostgreSQL, I performed data analysis to understand market potential, customer behavior, product performance, revenue trends, and rental costs.

The objective is to recommend the top three cities for new store expansion based on data-driven insights.

## Business Objectives

- Estimate potential coffee consumers across Indian cities.
- Analyze coffee sales and revenue performance.
- Identify top-selling coffee products.
- Understand customer distribution across cities.
- Evaluate rental costs against sales performance.
- Analyze monthly sales growth.
- Recommend the top three cities for physical store expansion.

## Database Structure

The project uses four tables:

1. **Cities** – City information, population, estimated rent and city ranking.
2. **Customers** – Customer details and city relationships.
3. **Products** – Product information and pricing.
4. **Sales** – Sales transactions, dates, totals and ratings.

## Key Business Insights

- Pune generated the highest total sales of approximately ₹1.26 million.
- Delhi has the largest estimated coffee consumer market at 7.75 million.
- Jaipur recorded the highest unique customer count with 69 customers.
- Pune demonstrated the highest average sales per customer.
- Product-level analysis identified the best-selling coffee products.

## Final Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the top three recommended cities for physical store expansion are:

1. **Pune** – Strong revenue performance and high average sales per customer.
2. **Delhi** – Large potential coffee consumer market and strong customer base.
3. **Jaipur** – Highest customer adoption and lower estimated rental overhead.
